Haggis Words word lists
=======================

answer-words.txt contains 2,309 words which can be selected as puzzles. Its
fixed shuffled order is the permanent family sequence: Word 1, Word 2 and so
on. Do not reorder this file without deliberately starting a new sequence.
allowed-words.txt contains 10,657 additional words accepted only as guesses.
The two lists do not overlap.

Sources:
https://github.com/Ephimoon/Wordle/blob/main/wordle-answers-2309.txt
https://github.com/Gregory-Meyer/wordle/blob/master/wordle-allowed-guesses.txt

Haggis Words is an independent family game and is not affiliated with Wordle
or The New York Times.
