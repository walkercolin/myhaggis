Poople word list
================

four-letter-words.txt contains four-letter English words connected to POOP by
one-letter changes. Common-word frequency is used only to prioritise friendly
starting words; all included words can be used during a ladder.

Word strings are derived from dwyl/english-words, released under the Unlicense:
https://github.com/dwyl/english-words

Common-word ordering uses Peter Norvig's published count_1w frequency data:
https://www.norvig.com/ngrams/
